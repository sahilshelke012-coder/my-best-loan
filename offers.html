Offer anatomy

Four Things to Check Before an Offer Feels Valuable.

The headline is only the start. A useful offer also makes its eligibility, validity and cost conditions easy to understand.

01

Headline Benefit

ROI reduction, processing-fee benefit or another clearly stated lender promotion.

02

Who It Applies To

Customer profile, income band, loan category, employer, asset or other eligibility condition.

03

Validity Window

When the offer starts, when it closes and whether sanction or disbursal must happen within the window.

04

Important Conditions

Documentation, credit profile, lender approval, additional charges and exclusions.
