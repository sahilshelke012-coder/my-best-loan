FAQ

Personal Loan
Questions,
Answered

Find clear answers to common questions about personal loans, including eligibility, application, interest rates, repayment terms, and required documents.

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for personal financial needs such as education, medical expenses, home improvements, travel, or other planned expenses. The loan amount and repayment terms depend on the lender and your eligibility.

Typically, you may need identity proof, address proof, income documents, bank statements, and employment or business-related documents. The exact requirements may vary depending on the lender and applicant profile.

Your monthly repayment generally depends on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure. Before proceeding, review the applicable interest rate, fees, tenure, and total repayment amount provided by the lender.
