LENDER EVALUATION

What Lenders May Evaluate

Lenders may review several aspects of your business and financial profile when considering a business loan application.

BUSINESS FACTORS

Lenders may consider your business history, time in operation, industry, revenue consistency, and overall business performance.

FINANCIAL FACTORS

Financial information such as revenue, expenses, cash flow, existing obligations, and repayment capacity may be reviewed.

CREDIT PROFILE

Your business and personal credit history may be considered along with previous borrowing and repayment records.

LOAN PURPOSE

Lenders may review how the requested funds will be used and whether the proposed financing aligns with your business needs.

PREPARATION

Prepare Your Business for Funding

Getting your business ready for funding can make the application process clearer and more efficient. Organize your business information, financial records, and funding requirements before applying.

DOCUMENTATION

Prepare essential business documents such as registration details, financial statements, bank records, tax information, and other documents that may be requested during the application process.

READINESS

Review your funding requirements, loan amount, repayment capacity, and business plans so you are prepared to discuss your financing needs with potential lenders.
