LOAN-WISE FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about different loan types, eligibility, documents, interest rates, and repayment.

A home loan is a financial facility that helps you purchase, construct, or renovate a residential property. The loan is generally repaid through monthly instalments over an agreed tenure.

The eligible loan amount depends on factors such as your income, credit profile, existing financial obligations, property value, and the lender's eligibility criteria.

Salaried and self-employed individuals who meet the lender's eligibility criteria can apply for a home loan. Eligibility may depend on income, age, credit history, employment, and repayment capacity.

The interest rate varies depending on the lender, loan amount, tenure, borrower profile, credit history, and prevailing lending terms.

Commonly required documents include identity proof, address proof, income documents, bank statements, property documents, and other documents requested by the lender.

Home loans are generally repaid through monthly EMIs over the selected loan tenure. You should review the repayment schedule and applicable charges with the lender before proceeding.
