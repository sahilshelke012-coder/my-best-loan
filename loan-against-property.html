FAQS

Loan Against Property Questions, Answered

Find clear answers to frequently asked questions about property-backed lending, eligibility, property types, and repayment terms.

A Loan Against Property is a secured loan where you mortgage your residential, commercial, or industrial property to access substantial funds while retaining property ownership and usage rights.

Most lenders accept self-occupied or rented residential homes, apartments, commercial offices, shops, and selected industrial properties, subject to clear title documents and lender valuation.

Eligible amounts usually range from 50% to 75% of the property's registered market value, depending on your income profile, credit score, property valuation, and lender norms.
