FAQs

Vehicle Loan Questions, Answered

Find clear answers to common questions about vehicle loans, eligibility, documents, repayment, and the application process.

A vehicle loan is financing that helps you purchase a new or used car, bike, or other eligible vehicle. The loan is repaid through scheduled installments according to the agreed terms.

Individuals who meet the lender's eligibility requirements can apply. Eligibility may depend on factors such as income, employment, credit profile, age, and the vehicle being financed.

Common documents include identity and address proof, income or employment documents, bank statements, and vehicle-related documents such as a quotation or purchase details.
