PARTNER ROLE

What Does a MyBestLoan Partner Do?

As a MyBestLoan partner, you help customers find suitable loan solutions, connect them with trusted lenders, and guide them through the loan application process. Earn commissions while building strong customer relationships.

ROLE POINT 1

Connect Customers with Loan Solutions

Understand customer needs, recommend suitable loan products, and connect borrowers with the right financial institutions.

ROLE POINT 2

Guide Customers Through the Process

Assist customers with documentation, explain loan requirements, and help them navigate the loan application process smoothly.

ROLE POINT 3

Earn Through Successful Referrals

Generate qualified loan leads, build lasting customer relationships, and earn commissions on successful loan referrals.

REFERRAL PROCESS

How the Referral Process Works

Start your partnership with My Best Loan and earn commissions by referring customers who need loan assistance. Follow these four simple steps to connect customers with suitable loan solutions.

1

Step 1 – Register as a Partner

Complete the partner registration process and provide the required details to join the My Best Loan partner network.

2

Step 2 – Refer Potential Customers

Identify customers looking for loans and share their details through the My Best Loan referral process.

3

Step 3 – Loan Application Assistance

Help customers understand loan requirements, submit necessary documents, and complete their loan applications.

4

Step 4 – Earn Your Commission

Receive commissions on successful referrals according to the applicable partner terms and conditions.
